Cash Transfer Schemes: Policy Evaluation

Examining the design, implementation and policy outcomes of cash-transfer interventions.

FOCUS AREA Social Protection
RESEARCH TYPE Research Brief
YEAR 2026

Overview

This research evaluates cash-transfer interventions by examining how programme design and implementation influence policy outcomes.

Research Focus

  • Targeting and beneficiary identification
  • Administrative capacity
  • Last-mile delivery
  • Digital access and exclusion
  • Policy implementation
  • Fiscal sustainability

Key Policy Issues

01 — Targeting

Programme outcomes depend significantly on how beneficiaries are identified and targeted.

02 — Delivery

Administrative and last-mile delivery constraints can limit the effectiveness of otherwise well-designed interventions.

03 — Digital Exclusion

Increasingly digital delivery systems create questions around accessibility and exclusion.

Policy Implications

Effective cash-transfer policy requires attention not only to the size and design of transfers, but also to targeting, institutional capacity and delivery mechanisms.
