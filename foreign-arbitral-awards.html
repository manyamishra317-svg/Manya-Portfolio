Strategic Challenges

01 — Appellate Delays

Although Section 50 limits appeals against enforcement orders, discretionary Special Leave Petitions (SLPs) to the Supreme Court under Article 136 of the Constitution can sometimes cause procedural delays.

02 — Asset Tracing

Identifying untainted assets of foreign judgment-debtors within Indian jurisdiction requires proactive investigation prior to filing.

Legal Architecture, Judicial Trajectory, and Practical Execution

An exhaustive analysis of the enforcement regime governing foreign arbitral awards in India. Governed primarily by Part II of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 ("A&C Act"), which incorporates the 1958 New York Convention and the 1927 Geneva Convention, the Indian enforcement procedure operates under a principle of minimal judicial intervention.
